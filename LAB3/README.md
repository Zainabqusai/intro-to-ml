EDA – Referral of Medical Cases 2025 Q1
Overview:
This project performs Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on the Referral of Medical Cases 2025 Q1 dataset to analyze healthcare referral patterns across regions, gender, age groups, and disease categories.
Objective:
The objective is to understand referral distribution, identify high-demand regions and disease codes, and support data-driven healthcare planning.
Analysis Performed:
Data inspection and cleaning.
Missing values and duplicates check.
Distribution of total referrals.
Referrals by gender, age group, and region.
Top 10 disease codes.
Correlation analysis (if applicable).
